## 🌱 Mental Health & Support

Serene Steps includes designated spaces for members who want to talk about difficult experiences.

These spaces are intended for **peer support**, not professional medical or crisis services.

Members are encouraged to:

- Respect other people's boundaries
- Use content warnings where appropriate
- Keep sensitive content in designated channels
- Avoid graphic descriptions
- Never encourage or glorify harmful behaviour
- Respect "no advice" and "no replies" requests
- Take breaks whenever they need one

You never have to respond to someone's vent, and you're always allowed to step away from support channels if you need some breathing room.

---

## 🛡️ Moderation

Serene Steps uses a flexible moderation system.

Depending on the situation, staff may use:

**Reminder → Warning → Mute → Kick → Ban**

Not every situation will follow every step.

Serious violations may result in stronger action immediately.

Staff may consider factors such as:

- Severity
- Intent
- Previous behaviour
- Whether the behaviour continued after being asked to stop
- Impact on other members
- Whether the behaviour was deliberate
