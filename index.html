🍫 The Basic Rules

These are the rules everyone in Serene Steps is expected to understand and follow.

They're intentionally simple here. If you want examples and detailed explanations, check the Detailed Rules tab.

01

🍫 Be Kind & Sweet

Treat other members with kindness and respect. Discrimination, hate speech, racism, harassment, and targeted cruelty are not allowed.

02

🌿 Keep It Cozy

Avoid unnecessary drama, public call-outs, baiting, and passive-aggressive behaviour.

03

🍵 Keep It PG-13

NSFW, sexually explicit, pornographic, excessively graphic, or otherwise inappropriate content is prohibited.

04

💬 No Spamming

Don't flood channels with messages, repeated content, excessive caps, emoji spam, or unnecessary tags.

05

📚 Stay On Topic

Use the correct channel for your conversation. Venting belongs in vent spaces, bot commands belong in bot channels, and so on.

06

🫶 Respect Boundaries & DMs

Ask before moving conversations into DMs and respect members who don't want private messages.

07

🚫 No Advertising

Self-promotion, server invites, advertising, and selling products without permission are not allowed.

08

🛡️ Listen to Staff

Follow reasonable instructions from moderators and administrators.

09

🎀 Keep Profiles Cute & Clean

Avatars, usernames, nicknames, bios, and statuses must remain appropriate.

10

🌪️ No Disruptions

Trolling, raiding, coordinated spam, mass disruption, and attempts to damage the server may result in an immediate ban.

🌿 Detailed Rules

This section explains what each rule actually means, gives examples of good and bad behaviour, and explains what may happen when a rule is broken.

01

🍫 Be Kind & Sweet

What this means

You don't have to like everyone or agree with everyone. You do need to communicate without attacking or degrading other people.

Harassment can include repeated insults, deliberately targeting someone, encouraging others to gang up on someone, or continuing behaviour after someone has asked you to stop.

What to do

  • Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
  • Give people space when they ask for it.
  • Report serious problems to staff.
  • Keep jokes from becoming personal attacks.

What not to do

  • Use slurs or hateful insults.
  • Make discriminatory jokes.
  • Encourage people to gang up on someone.
  • Repeatedly insult someone and call it a joke.
  • Continue bothering someone after they say stop.
🌿 Good example "I don't really agree with your opinion, but I understand where you're coming from."
🚫 Not okay "You're stupid for believing that."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Minor incidents may result in a reminder, message removal, or warning. Repeated harassment or serious hateful behaviour may result in a mute, kick, or ban. Severe violations may skip the normal progression.

02

🌿 Keep It Cozy

What this means

Arguments happen. Turning an argument into a public spectacle is different.

Public call-outs, vague posts aimed at specific members, baiting, and trying to make other people pick sides can quickly make the server uncomfortable.

What not to do

  • Start public arguments for attention.
  • Make posts clearly aimed at another member.
  • Encourage people to pick sides.
  • Bring old conflicts into unrelated conversations.
  • Follow someone around the server to argue.
🌿 Better "I think there was a misunderstanding. I'm going to step away for now and ask staff if we need help."
🚫 Not okay "@everyone LOOK AT WHAT [member] DID."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Staff may ask you to stop, remove messages, issue a warning, or use stronger moderation if the behaviour continues.

03

🍵 Keep Serene Steps PG-13

What this means

Serene Steps is intended to be a general-audience community. Content should remain appropriate for a PG-13 environment.

This applies to messages, images, videos, links, embeds, profiles, usernames, and statuses.

Not allowed

  • Sexually explicit or pornographic content.
  • Graphic injury or violence imagery.
  • Sexualised content involving minors.
  • Attempts to bypass the rule with spoilers or links.
🌿 Okay "That horror movie was really creepy!"
🚫 Not okay Posting explicit material and claiming it is allowed because it was hidden behind a spoiler.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Inappropriate content may be removed immediately. Depending on severity, staff may warn, mute, kick, or ban.

04

💬 No Spamming

What this means

Don't flood a channel with rapid messages, repeated content, excessive caps, emoji spam, or unnecessary tags.

🌿 Okay "GUESS WHAT!! I finally finished my drawing!"
🚫 Not okay "HI HI HI HI HI HI HI" followed by repeated unnecessary mentions.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Staff may ask you to slow down, remove spam, or issue a warning. Deliberate or repeated spam may lead to a mute or stronger action.

05

📚 Stay On Topic

What this means

Channels exist for specific purposes. Please use the appropriate channel for your message.

  • Vent content → designated vent channels.
  • Bot commands → bot channels.
  • Art → art channels.
  • Media → media channels.
🌿 Better "I'll move this conversation to #general so we don't fill the art channel with unrelated chat."
🚫 Not okay Deliberately starting an unrelated argument in a channel after being asked to move.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Usually you'll simply be redirected to the correct channel. Repeated refusal can lead to a warning or further action.

06

🫶 Respect Boundaries & DMs

What this means

Being friendly does not automatically mean someone wants private messages. Ask before moving a conversation into DMs when appropriate.

If someone says their DMs are closed, asks you not to message them, or stops responding, respect their space.

🌿 Good "Would you be okay with me DMing you about this?"
🚫 Not okay "Please don't DM me."

"I'm going to DM you anyway."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Depending on severity, staff may warn, mute, kick, or ban. Repeatedly contacting someone through alternate accounts after they have asked you to stop can result in stronger action.

07

🚫 No Advertising

What this means

Don't use Serene Steps to recruit members, advertise other servers, promote products, or send unsolicited promotional messages.

🚫 Not okay "Everyone join my server! discord.gg/..."
🌿 Better Ask staff whether there is an approved place or system for sharing a project.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Advertisements may be removed. Depending on intent and repetition, staff may warn, mute, kick, or ban.

08

🛡️ Listen to Staff

What this means

Staff are responsible for keeping the server safe, organised, and welcoming. If staff ask you to stop, move a conversation, or remove something, please cooperate.

🌿 Good Staff: "Please move this discussion to #media."
Member: "Sure, sorry about that!"
🚫 Not okay Staff: "Please stop the argument."
Member: "No. You can't tell me what to do."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Refusing reasonable staff instructions can result in a warning, mute, kick, or ban depending on the circumstances.

09

🎀 Keep Profiles Cute & Clean

What this means

Avatars, nicknames, usernames, bios, and statuses are all part of your presence in the server. They must remain appropriate.

🚫 Not okay Changing your nickname to hateful or offensive material after being asked to change it.
🌿 Better Choose a cute nickname that fits the chocomint atmosphere! 🍫🌿
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Staff may ask you to change the offending profile element. Refusal may lead to moderation action.

10

🌪️ No Disruptions, Raiding, or Trolling

What this means

Do not deliberately attempt to make the server unusable or encourage other people to disrupt it.

  • No raids.
  • No coordinated spam.
  • No mass-pinging.
  • No deliberate channel disruption.
  • No attempts to damage server systems.
🚫 Not okay "Everyone join Serene Steps and spam every channel!"
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Raiding, malicious disruption, or coordinated attacks may result in an immediate and permanent ban.

💭 Mental Health & Support

Serene Steps has vent and support spaces because we want members to have somewhere they can talk about difficult feelings.

These spaces have additional rules so that people can share difficult experiences without exposing others to unnecessarily graphic, harmful, or overwhelming content.

Remember: You are never required to respond to a vent, and you are allowed to take a break from support channels.

11

💭 Peer Support, Not Professional Help

Serene Steps is a peer community. Members can listen and support one another, but the server cannot provide therapy, diagnosis, medical treatment, or professional crisis intervention.

🌿 Good "I'm sorry you're going through this. I can't diagnose what's happening, but I hope you can talk to someone you trust."
🚫 Not okay "You definitely have a disorder. You don't need a professional because I know exactly what you have."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Unsafe or misleading advice may be removed. Repeatedly presenting yourself as a professional or giving dangerous advice may result in moderation action.

12

⚠️ Trigger Warnings & Spoilers

Sensitive topics should be labelled clearly and potentially triggering details should be hidden behind Discord spoiler formatting where appropriate.

🌿 Good "TW: discussion of self-harm"

||I have been struggling with urges and it's been difficult emotionally.||
🚫 Not okay Posting upsetting details first and putting "TW" at the very end.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Staff may ask you to edit or remove the message. Repeated problems may result in a warning or stronger moderation.

13

🕊️ No Graphic Descriptions

You may talk about difficult experiences, but do not provide detailed or graphic descriptions of self-harm, eating disorders, violence, injuries, or other disturbing material.

🌿 Good "I've been having a really difficult day and could use some kindness."
🚫 Not okay Posting detailed descriptions of injuries or instructions about harmful behaviour.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Graphic or instructional content may be removed immediately. Serious violations may result in stronger moderation.

14

🌱 No Glorification or Encouragement of Harm

Talking honestly about struggling is different from encouraging or romanticising harmful behaviour.

Do not turn suffering into a competition or make someone feel like they need to prove how badly they are struggling.

🌿 Good "You don't have to prove how bad things are to deserve support."
🚫 Not okay "You should keep doing it; it makes you stronger."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Encouragement or glorification may result in immediate message removal and moderation. Serious or repeated behaviour may result in a mute, kick, or ban.

15

🫶 Respect Vent Preferences

If someone says "no advice," listen instead of giving advice. If they say "no replies," give them space.

🌿 Good Member: "Vent — no advice please."
Response: "That sounds really hard. I'm here to listen. 🫶"
🚫 Not okay "You NEED to do this. Here's a list of everything you should be doing."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Staff may ask you to stop or remove replies. Repeatedly ignoring vent preferences may result in a warning or other action.

16

🍵 Keep Heavy Vents in Vent Channels

Deeply sensitive or triggering content belongs in designated vent/support spaces rather than general chat.

🌿 Better "I'll put this in the vent channel so people can choose whether they're in the headspace to read it."
🚫 Not okay Posting a highly sensitive vent in general chat without using the designated support space.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Staff will normally redirect the conversation. Repeatedly ignoring channel boundaries may result in a warning.

17

🛡️ Crisis & Emergency Safety

Serene Steps is not equipped to manage emergencies or provide crisis intervention.

Members may talk about struggling within the support rules, but the server cannot safely become someone's emergency response system.

Do not

  • Ask members to become your only source of emergency help.
  • Promise another member that you can keep them safe.
  • Act as a crisis professional if you are not one.
  • Encourage someone to hide an emergency from trusted people.
  • Post instructions for harmful behaviour.

Do

  • Tell a trusted person in real life when you need help.
  • Use appropriate professional or emergency support when necessary.
  • Tell staff if you are seriously concerned about a member.
  • Step away if a situation becomes overwhelming.
🌿 Better "I'm having a really difficult time. I'm going to tell someone I trust because I don't think I should handle this alone."
🚫 Not okay "Nobody else needs to know. You have to stay online with me and keep me safe."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Staff may remove unsafe content, intervene, and direct the situation toward appropriate real-world support.

18

💚 No Guilt-Tripping

Nobody owes another member a reply, emotional labour, or constant availability.

🌿 Good "That's okay! Take care of yourself. 🫶"
🚫 Not okay "Wow. I guess you don't care about me because you won't reply."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Staff may intervene and ask the behaviour to stop. Repeated or serious guilt-tripping may result in moderation action.

19

🔒 Protect Member Privacy

Do not share another member's private information, DMs, vents, personal experiences, or identifying information without permission.

🌿 Good Keep private conversations private unless there is a legitimate moderation or safety reason for staff to see them.
🚫 Not okay Taking a private conversation and posting it elsewhere to embarrass someone.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Privacy violations can result in serious moderation action, including a ban depending on severity.

20

🌱 Know Your Limits

You are never required to read or respond to vent content.

If vent channels negatively affect you, you may mute them or remove the Vent Access role through <#channel-roles> if that option is available.

🌿 Good "I think I need a break from vents for a while, so I'm going to mute the channel."
🚫 Not okay "You can't leave the vent channel because we need you to listen."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Members who pressure or shame others for setting boundaries may receive moderation.

🛡️ Staff & Reports

Staff are here to help keep Serene Steps welcoming, organised, and safe. If something goes wrong, please report it rather than trying to handle it yourself.

21

⚖️ How Moderation Works

🍃

Reminder

Staff explain the rule and ask you to stop or change something.

🍵

Warning

A formal warning may be recorded for a violation.

🌿

Mute

Your ability to communicate may temporarily be restricted.

🍫

Kick

You may be removed from the server.

🚫

Ban

You may be permanently removed.

This is a general guideline rather than a promise that every case will follow every step.

Severe behaviour can skip directly to stronger action.

22

📩 How to Report Something

If a rule is broken or you feel unsafe, report the situation to staff privately.

Useful information

  • Who was involved.
  • What happened.
  • Where it happened.
  • When it happened.
  • Screenshots or message links when appropriate.
🌿 Good report "Hi, I'd like to report something that happened in #general. I have screenshots if they're needed."
🚫 Not okay "EVERYONE REPORT THIS PERSON RIGHT NOW!"
23

🛑 Please Don't Mini-Mod

Members can point someone toward the rules kindly, but members should not act as unofficial staff.

🌿 Better "I think this might be against the rules. I'll let staff know."
🚫 Not okay "I'm basically staff, so you're banned."
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Repeated mini-modding may result in a warning or other moderation, especially if it becomes harassment or disruption.

24

🍃 Staff Decisions & Appeals

You may disagree with a moderation decision, but please discuss disagreements privately and calmly.

Do

  • Ask privately for clarification.
  • Use an appeal system if one exists.
  • Explain your side respectfully.

Don't

  • Start a public argument.
  • Rally members against staff.
  • Threaten staff.
  • Spam staff demanding an immediate response.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Public disruption, harassment, or refusal to follow reasonable staff instructions may lead to moderation action.

25

🎟️ Tickets & Confidential Reports

Use tickets or private staff messages for sensitive reports instead of posting personal information publicly.

Do

  • Explain what happened clearly.
  • Provide relevant evidence.
  • Tell staff if you're concerned about privacy.
  • Be patient while staff investigate.

Don't

  • Fabricate evidence.
  • Make deliberately false reports.
  • Spam multiple staff members with the same report.
  • Use reports to harass another member.
⚠️ What happens if you disobey?

Deliberately false reports, fabricated evidence, or misuse of the ticket system may result in moderation action.

⚖️ Enforcement & Consequences

We want moderation to be fair and understandable. Not every mistake deserves the same response.

1

🍃 Reminders

For minor mistakes or misunderstandings, staff may simply explain the rule and ask you to stop.

A reminder is not necessarily a formal warning. It is often simply a chance to correct the behaviour.

2

🍵 Warnings

A formal warning may be given when a rule is clearly broken, repeated, or ignored after a reminder.

Warnings help staff keep track of repeated behaviour and determine when stronger action may be necessary.

3

🌿 Mutes

A mute temporarily prevents a member from communicating in some or all server channels.

Mutes may be used when someone needs time to cool down, when behaviour continues after warnings, or when immediate disruption needs to stop.

4

🍫 Kicks

A kick removes a member from the server. Depending on the circumstances, they may be allowed to return.

5

🚫 Bans

A ban permanently removes someone from the community unless staff decide otherwise.

Serious harassment, raids, severe hateful behaviour, deliberate disruption, serious privacy violations, or other major violations may result in an immediate ban.

!

🌱 Remember: Mistakes Happen

Nobody expects members to be perfect.

If you make a genuine mistake:

  1. Listen when someone corrects you.
  2. Stop the behaviour.
  3. Apologise when appropriate.
  4. Learn from it.
  5. Move forward.

At the same time, serious or repeated behaviour can still have consequences.
